Getting to Rio Bella

Rio Bella Resort sits along the Frio River in Leakey, deep in the Texas Hill Country. It’s an easy drive from either airport — the final stretch winds through Frio Canyon, so give yourself daylight if you can.

Before you pack

Good to know

November weather

Hill Country falls are mild — warm, sunny afternoons and cool evenings. Bring a layer for after dark by the river.

What to pack

Something for the water — river shoes, swimwear, a towel — alongside your wedding attire. Nights can dip, so pack a wrap or jacket. Bring hiking clothes if you plan to stay for the Sunday activities.

Cell service

Signal is spotty in the canyon. Download your directions before you leave the highway, and tell folks you may be off-grid for a bit.

Getting around

You’ll want a car for the weekend. Everything centers on Rio Bella, but the nearest towns and shops are a short drive away.
